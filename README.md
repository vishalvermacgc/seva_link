# 🚀 SevaLink AI

### Bridging Scattered Community Data to Real-Time Volunteer Action

SevaLink AI is an **AI-powered community response platform** that transforms unstructured NGO field data into actionable insights, identifies urgent needs, and connects them with the right volunteers in real time.

---

## 📌 Problem

NGOs often rely on **paper-based surveys, field reports, spreadsheets, and fragmented data sources** to understand community needs. This creates several challenges:

* 🚨 Urgent needs are difficult to identify quickly
* 📄 Unstructured data is difficult to analyze and act upon
* 👥 Volunteer skills and availability are not utilized efficiently
* ⏱️ Delays between identifying a need and taking action can impact communities

There is a need for a system that can turn **raw community data into immediate, actionable response**.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

**SevaLink AI** uses AI to bridge the gap between **data collection and volunteer action**.

The platform analyzes NGO reports and survey data to:

* 🧠 Extract critical information from unstructured reports
* 📍 Identify the **Need, Location, and Urgency**
* ⚡ Convert identified needs into actionable tasks
* 👥 Match tasks with suitable volunteers based on their skills
* 🔔 Deliver real-time task notifications to volunteers

> **From scattered data → structured insights → actionable tasks → real-world impact.**

---

## ⚙️ How It Works

```text
NGO Field Reports / Survey Data
              ↓
        Google Gemini AI
              ↓
   Need • Location • Urgency
              ↓
          Firebase
              ↓
     Task Generation
              ↓
      Skill-Based Matching
              ↓
   Real-Time Volunteer Alerts
              ↓
        Community Action
```

### Workflow

1. **Data Collection**
   NGOs upload field reports, surveys, or community observations.

2. **AI-Powered Extraction**
   Gemini AI processes the unstructured data and extracts key information such as:

   * Type of need
   * Location
   * Urgency level

3. **Centralized Storage**
   Structured data is securely stored and synchronized using Firebase.

4. **Task Generation**
   Identified needs are converted into actionable volunteer tasks.

5. **Intelligent Matching**
   Volunteers are matched based on their skills and task requirements.

6. **Real-Time Action**
   Volunteers receive instant task updates and notifications, enabling faster response.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🧠 AI Data Extraction

Convert messy, unstructured field reports into structured and actionable information using Google Gemini.

### 🔥 Urgency Heatmap

Visualize high-need locations and identify areas requiring immediate attention.

### 👥 Skill-Based Volunteer Matching

Connect each task with volunteers whose skills are most relevant to the requirement.

### ⚡ Real-Time Synchronization

Firebase enables real-time updates across NGO dashboards and volunteer task feeds.

### 📊 NGO Command Center

A centralized dashboard for NGOs to monitor community needs, manage tasks, and track volunteer responses.

### 📱 Volunteer Task Feed

Volunteers can view relevant tasks, receive updates, and respond to community needs in real time.
